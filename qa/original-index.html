Standards and institutional information

Professional practice, programme guidance and accountability.

These summaries explain the principles that guide participation in AIMS programmes. Detailed approved regulations may be obtained from the Secretariat.

Programme information

  • Entry: ND, HND, Degree and Graduate participants are placed at the corresponding certification stage.
  • Duration: Each certification stage runs for one academic year.
  • Learning: Lectures, assignments, individual and group projects, and prescribed examinations.
  • Assessment: Successful completion of all required coursework and examinations.
  • Certification: Certificates are issued to successful participants at the end of the session.
  • Fees: Confirm the current approved fee with the AIMS Office before making payment.

Vision, mission and values

Vision: To become Africa’s foremost professional institute for Information Management and rank among the best globally.

Mission: To advance knowledge, research and Information Management practice for the benefit of stakeholders throughout the economy.

Professional values: Knowledge, service, excellence, integrity, continuous development and responsible information practice.

Professional conduct

Participants are expected to act honestly, respect intellectual property, protect confidential information and uphold the reputation of the profession.

Assessment and certification

Certification depends on completing prescribed assignments, projects and examinations. Academic misconduct may affect eligibility for certification.

Continuing development

AIMS encourages members and certificate holders to maintain their competence through continuing learning, seminars, conferences and professional engagement.

Privacy notice

Application and enquiry information is collected only to respond, administer programmes, communicate relevant updates and maintain necessary institutional records. Information is not to be sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Complaints and appeals

Programme, assessment or service concerns should be submitted in writing to the AIMS Office for acknowledgement, review and an official response.

Equal opportunity

Programme access and professional development opportunities should be administered fairly, subject to applicable entry requirements and available capacity.
